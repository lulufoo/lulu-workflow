# Product Blueprint (Topic) — Section KW Criteria

Topic-level product architecture shaping only — not feature PRD or technical architecture.

---

## SI

| KW | Verifiable intent attributes |
|----|------------------------------|
| KW0 | Cannot be named |
| KW1 | Can state "what triggered this product exploration and what the current product situation is" |
| KW2 | Can state "trigger, current situation, and cost of unresolved product uncertainty — all three present and self-contained" |
| KW3 | Can state "the intended topic-level product direction is named without mixing BD/PS/PR content" |
| KW4 | Can state "a reviewer can judge direction readiness without follow-up on context" |

## BD

| KW | Verifiable intent attributes |

## PS

| KW | Verifiable intent attributes |
