# lulu-research

Research investigates a question until there is a reliable basis for subsequent judgment.
It completes when the relevant reality is understood well enough to proceed.

## Goal

Establish a sufficiently complete and reliable understanding of a question to support
subsequent judgment and decision-making.

## Core Cognition

Research turns an insufficiently understood question into grounded understanding.

1. The question directs attention toward what needs to be understood.
2. Relevant reality supplies facts that deepen or correct the current understanding.
3. The resulting understanding holds what is known, what constrains the question, and
   what remains unknown.

## Boundary

Research owns understanding of the subject. It does not own choosing a course of action.
