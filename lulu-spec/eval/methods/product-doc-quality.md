# Product Document Quality Method

**Role:** EvalMethod template for `lulu-spec` dimension `product-doc-quality`.

## Boundary

Audit the EvalTarget `product-doc.md` at product altitude. Do not require or
accept implementation detail such as file paths, APIs, stack choices, test
commands, ownership tables, or implementation order.

## Procedure

Evaluate each dimension independently, then perform one open-ended final read.

### Lens Substance

1. **Opening Arc:** problem, timing, and observable goal are reviewable.
2. **User Grounding:** primary users and an interpretable usage situation exist.
3. **Capability and Surfaces:** in-scope capabilities and visible objects are
   enumerable without technical detail.
4. **Flow and Boundaries:** one main path and protected exclusions are clear.
5. **Acceptance Closure:** done conditions are observable and trace to goal.

### Cross-Lens Integrity

6. **Internal Consistency:** sections do not contradict on capability, user, or
   outcome.
7. **Unambiguity and Terminology:** scope, actors, and object names admit one
   reasonable interpretation.
8. **Decision Traceability:** goals and scope stay within upstream decision
   boundaries without silent expansion.

### Sign-off Executability

9. **Observability of Done:** stakeholders can judge goal achievement without
   opening a technical plan.
10. **Explicit N/A and Assumptions:** thin surfaces and product assumptions are
    declared without forcing technical feasibility decisions.
11. **Scope Protectors:** exclusions make adjacent out-of-scope work clear.

### Non-Happy Visibility

12. **Empty and Failure Visibility:** common empty or key-failure outcomes are
    visible at product altitude or explicitly deferred.

## Finding Priority

Prioritize decision traceability, opening arc, acceptance closure, scope
protectors, and internal consistency before prose polish.
