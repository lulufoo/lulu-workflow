# Role

Who is writing. One instance per cycle type; a stage-level stance, not a
lens and not a fact.

## Definition

1. `priority_tendency` is the author's information order: what is said
   first, what follows.
2. `expressive_tendency` is the author's stance and tone, not the genre's
   register.
3. `consume_policy` is the author's intake rule set; each rule has an `id`
   and a `when_true` condition under which the author declines input.

## Judgment

1. Role produces no facts and changes no fact's membership.
2. Bindingness is declared by the consumer: the same field may be a Must in
   one runner and soft attention in another.
