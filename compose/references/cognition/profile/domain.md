# Domain

What genre this stage produces. One instance per cycle type; a stage-level
stance, not a lens and not a fact.

## Definition

1. `cognitive_frame` (D1) is the analytical frame this stage sees problems
   through.
2. `intent_anchor` (D2) is the upstream intent this stage locks to and may
   not overturn.
3. `expression_conventions` are the genre's expressive norms, four keys:
   `register`, `carriers`, `scannability`, `altitude`.
4. `vocabulary_domain` is the genre's vocabulary, not the author's voice.
5. `audience_type` names who reads the document and what they must be able
   to do afterwards.

## Judgment

1. Domain produces no facts and changes no fact's membership.
2. Bindingness is declared by the consumer: the same field may be a Must in
   one runner and soft attention in another.
