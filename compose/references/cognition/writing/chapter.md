# Chapter

What a chapter is and what its body owes to its facts.

## Definition

1. A chapter is one narrative-arc leaf × one lens × the facts placed
   there. It is the unit of writing.
2. A fact is placed exactly once: one leaf, one chapter, under one lens
   drawn from its lens tags.
3. A chapter's id (`cid`) is `{leaf_id}-{lens}`; it names the chapter in
   the arc, the write state, and the assembled document alike.
4. The narrative arc (`narrative-arc.md`) is the topology: it decides
   where chapters sit and what titles they carry. Chapter order comes from
   the arc, not from the lens chain.

## Body obligations

1. Content ⊆ the chapter's facts. The body states nothing the facts do
   not, and does not restate another chapter's propositions.
2. Every anchor of a placed fact appears in the body as its concrete
   token; a hypernym does not count.
3. Where substance is missing, the body marks the gap plainly instead of
   filling it.
