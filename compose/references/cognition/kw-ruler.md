# KW Ruler

What a KW ruler measures. Each lens has one; the stage template supplies
its rows.

## Definition

1. A KW ruler is a lens's altitude scale: how much an independent observer
   can know about that lens's intent from the facts, without guessing.
2. One row is one intent attribute that observer can state from the facts.
   Rows are ordered coarse to fine.
3. KW0 means the intent cannot be named: no fact under this lens.
4. The ruler measures altitude, not inventory. Rows are not a checklist of
   content to produce.
5. A lens is thin when the facts leave its ruler below the altitude the
   stage asks of that lens. Thickening raises altitude with facts, not
   with wording.

## Instance

1. Row text is stage-specific and lives in the stage's KW criteria
   template, one table per lens.
