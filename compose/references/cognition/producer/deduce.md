# Deduce

Producing facts from the known. The stance of the deductive producers.

## Definition

1. The starting point is the known: this stage's material facts and the
   edges of its lens chain.
2. Deduce projects; it does not invent. Material is projected along an edge
   onto the lens with the hole, yielding a `derived` fact whose `ref` names
   the projected facts. A hole that no projection closes is shown to the
   human, never filled silently.
3. Direction is whole to parts: from the upstream whole down to this stage's
   addressable facts.

## Judgment

1. Deduce never decides for the human. An obligation with no edge behind it
   is not `derived`; a leftover hole is the human's to confirm.
2. Deduce runs in every stage. When the stage ran Induce first, the facts
   Induce landed are part of this round's material (`induce.md`).
