# Induce

Producing facts from the unknown. The stance of the inductive producers.

## Definition

1. The starting point is the unknown: what the fact set does not state.
   Unknown is neither false nor decided elsewhere.
2. Induce asks; it does not answer. An unknown becomes an Open — one
   question that matters to the slice. A human decides it. A landed Open
   expands into one or more `discovered` facts.
3. Direction is parts to whole: from slice evidence upward to a settled
   picture of the slice.

## Judgment

1. Induce never decides for the human. It surfaces the question, gathers
   evidence, and lays out options; landing, deferring, and rejecting are the
   human's acts. Deferred and rejected Opens leave the fact set unchanged.
2. Facts it lands join the one slice fact set and become material for
   Deduce (`deduce.md`).
